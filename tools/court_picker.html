Paste that into Play volume on the annotator's Target screen. Coordinates are fractions of the frame — 0,0 top-left, 1,1 bottom-right — so they stay correct if the video is re-encoded at another resolution.

Four corners is the usual case. On wide-angle GoPro footage the lens bows straight lines, so if a four-point quadrilateral visibly misses the bowed edges, keep clicking along them — the schema takes any number of points from three up.

Mark the play volume, not the painted lines: include the run-off you want the segmenter to treat as this court.
